# Booking widget (bg-book)
File: js/bg-book.js + css/bg-book.css, book.php (PHP + the CRM's database: bg_calendars, bg_cal_hours, bg_cal_services, bg_cal_closed,
bg_cal_bookings - made by 🗂 CRM ▸ 🗄 Create the site's database). Markup: <div data-bg-book data-calendar="bookings"></div>
(data-calendar = the calendar's slug; "bookings" is the one the CRM makes). It shows the services, a date, the free times and a short
form (name, email, phone, notes). The times, opening hours, days ahead, notice and "how many at once" are checked again on the server.
Pages: booking.html.
