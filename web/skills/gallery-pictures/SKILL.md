# Picture gallery (bg-gallery)
Files: css/bg-gallery.css, js/bg-gallery.js. Markup: div.bg-gallery > a[href=big picture][title=caption] > img[src=thumbnail][alt].
The thumbnail size is --bg-thumb on the gallery. Or <div class="bg-gallery" data-bg-json="data/gallery.json"></div> with
[{"src": "...", "thumb": "...", "caption": "..."}]. Put the pictures in images/.
