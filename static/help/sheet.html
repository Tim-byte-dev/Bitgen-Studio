▦ Spreadsheet

Tables, sums and lists in bitGen - a workbook of sheets with formulas that work themselves out again as you type. The ▦ button on the top bar opens and closes the docker. Workbooks are kept in your project's sheets folder (a .bgs file); what you are typing is also kept in the browser, so closing the docker loses nothing.

Typing

  • Click a cell and type. Enter keeps it and goes down, Tab goes right, Esc takes the typing back. F2 or a double-click changes what is in the cell.
  • Arrows move; Shift + arrows (or a drag) pick a block; Ctrl + arrows jump to the end of the data; Home goes to column A, Page Up / Down 20 rows; Ctrl+A picks everything; Delete empties the cells picked.
  • Numbers are understood as you type them: $1.50 becomes 1.5 shown as money, 25% is 0.25 shown as a percent, 17/11/2026 (day first) or 2026-11-17 is a date.
  • The ƒx bar above the grid shows what is really in the cell (the formula, not its answer) - you can type there too.

Formulas

  • Start with =: =B2*C2, =SUM(D2:D20), =IF(D2>100,"big","small"). + - * / ^, & joins text, = <> < > <= >= compare.
  • While typing a formula, click a cell (or drag over a block) to put its address in.
  • $A$1 keeps a reference fixed when the formula is copied or filled; A1 moves with it.
  • Another sheet: =Sheet2!A1 or ='My costs'!B4. Whole columns and rows: =SUM(B:B), =SUM(3:3).
  • ƒ▾ lists the functions: sums and counts (SUM, AVERAGE, MEDIAN, MIN, MAX, COUNT, COUNTA, COUNTBLANK, SUMIF(S), COUNTIF(S), AVERAGEIF), maths (ROUND, ROUNDUP, ROUNDDOWN, INT, ABS, SQRT, POWER, MOD, PRODUCT), logic (IF, IFS, IFERROR, AND, OR, NOT), text (LEN, TRIM, UPPER, LOWER, LEFT, RIGHT, MID, CONCAT, TEXT, VALUE), dates (TODAY, NOW, DATE, YEAR, MONTH, DAY) and look-ups (VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP, INDEX, MATCH).
  • Errors show in the cell: #DIV/0! (divided by 0), #REF! (a cell that was deleted), #NAME? (a function it does not know), #CYCLE! (a formula that needs its own answer).

Filling, copying, moving

  • The small square at the corner of the picked cells is the fill handle: drag it down or across to copy - formulas move their references, and numbers or dates in a row of two or more carry on the series (1, 2 → 3, 4, 5).
  • Ctrl+C / Ctrl+X / Ctrl+V copy, cut and paste - inside the sheet formulas move with the paste; tables copied from another program (text with tabs) paste into the cells.
  • Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo and redo.
  • Right-click a cell for Insert / Delete rows and columns - formulas everywhere follow the cells they pointed at. Drag the edge of a column letter or row number to size it.

Looks - ▤ Format Cells (Ctrl+1)

  • ▤ Format… on the toolbar, Ctrl+1 or a right-click on the cells opens Format Cells. What you change there goes on every selected cell (the rest of each cell's look stays); one Ctrl+Z takes it back.
  • Number: General, Number (decimals, 1,000 separator, how negative numbers show - with a minus, red, in brackets), Currency ($, A$, €, £, ¥, ฿ …), Accounting (the sign at the cell's edge, decimal points lined up, a dash for zero), Date, Time, Percentage, Fraction, Scientific, Text (what is typed stays as typed - 0012 keeps its zeros), Special (postcodes, ABN, phone numbers) and Custom - type any format code: 0 # ? digits, , thousands, %, d m y h s and AM/PM, [h] hours past 24, [Red] a colour, "text", and up to four sections positive;negative;zero;text or conditions like [>=1000]. The sample shows the active cell as you choose.
  • Alignment: left / centre / right with an indent, fill, justify, centre across selection (a title over several columns without merging), distributed; top / centre / bottom; wrap text (the row grows to fit), shrink to fit, merge cells, text direction, and orientation - drag the dial or type the degrees (-90 to 90), or stack the letters one under another.
  • Font: the font (this computer's fonts too), regular / italic / bold, size (bigger text makes the row taller), underline (single, double, accounting), colour, strikethrough, superscript, subscript.
  • Border: pick a line (13 styles) and a colour, then a preset (None, Outline, Inside lines), an edge button, or click the edge in the preview - the diagonals too. Click an edge again to take it off.
  • Fill: a colour from the palette (or any colour), two-colour shading (across, down, diagonal, from the centre), or a pattern with its own colour.
  • Protection: Locked and Hidden count once the sheet is protected - tick Protect this sheet, or right-click the sheet's tab ▸ 🔒 Protect sheet. Then locked cells can't be changed (every cell starts locked - untick Locked on the cells people should still fill in) and a hidden cell's formula isn't shown; 🔓 Unprotect sheet opens it again.
  • The toolbar has the quick ones: the Number Format list, .0← / .00→ one decimal fewer / more, B I U, text and fill colour (⊘ = no fill), left / centre / right, wrap, borders ▾ and ⊞ merge. A number too wide for its column shows ##### - widen the column.

Excel files, charts and ✨ Dashboard

  • ⤒ Import opens an Excel workbook (.xlsx) or a CSV file: every sheet, values, formulas, number formats, fonts, fills, borders, merged cells, column widths and frozen panes. A formula with a function bitGen does not have yet keeps the result the file was saved with. ⤓ Export ▾ saves the workbook as .xlsx (downloaded, and kept in the project's sheets folder) or this sheet as CSV.
  • 📊 Chart ▾ makes a chart (column, bar, line, area, pie, doughnut, scatter) of the selected cells - the first row gives the series' names, the first column the categories. Drag it to move it, drag its corner to size it, double-click it to change its title, type or data, Delete removes it. Charts follow rows inserted above them and are kept in the workbook (an .xlsx gets their data, not the charts).
  • ✨ Dashboard - the LLM (the prompt docker's) looks at your data's columns - their names, what kind of values they hold, a few examples and the totals, not every row - and plans a dashboard; type what you want to see if you like ("sales by region and month"). bitGen builds it on a new first sheet: headline numbers in tiles, breakdowns with their tables and charts, a trend over time - all live formulas, so they follow the data - and formats the data as a table (header, banded rows, number formats, widths, a filter, the header kept on screen). Pick An Excel or CSV file… to start from a file. Untick Use the LLM (or when no LLM answers) and the dashboard is planned from the data's own shape. One Ctrl+Z takes it all back.

✨ AI in the cells

  • ✨ AI ▾ ▸ ✍ Write a formula… - say what it should work out ("the total of Amount where Region is North"). The LLM (the prompt docker's) is told the table's columns - headings, what they hold, a few values - not every row, and answers with a formula. bitGen works it out before it goes in: you see its result, can change it, then Put it in. A formula for one row offers Fill down the table's rows.
  • 💡 Explain this formula (also on a cell's right-click menu) - every cell and range the formula reads with what is in it now, then the LLM's explanation in plain words.
  • 🪄 Fill from examples (Ctrl+E) - type what one or two cells of a new column should hold, then Ctrl+E: bitGen finds the rule itself when it can (words of the other cells, the part before / after @ , - and other marks, joined, UPPER / lower / Proper case, initials) and fills the empty rows - no LLM needed. When no rule like that fits (a sum, a lookup…), the LLM writes a formula, and it is used only when it gives every example; if none does, the LLM fills the values and says they are its guess. One Ctrl+Z takes a fill back.
  • New functions in ƒ▾: PROPER, FIND, SEARCH, SUBSTITUTE, REPLACE, REPT, EXACT, TEXTJOIN, AVERAGEIFS, MAXIFS, MINIFS, SUMPRODUCT, LARGE, SMALL, RANK, ROWS, COLUMNS, CEILING, FLOOR, WEEKDAY, HOUR, MINUTE, EDATE, EOMONTH, DATEDIF, CHOOSE, IFNA, ISERROR, ISNA, ISBLANK, ISNUMBER, ISTEXT, SWITCH.

📌 Constants - amounts that repeat

  • 📌 Constants keeps amounts that come or go again and again - rent, wages, a phone plan, a loan: Type (its name), Amount, Every (day, week, fortnight, month, quarter, year), Start date, Description, Expense (paid) or Income (received), and an Ends date if it stops.
  • When the amount changes, add the same name again with the new amount and the date it starts (click the constant in the list to fill the form). The earlier amount stays for the dates before it - rent at 540 a week for a year, then 580, gives 52 entries at 540 and 580 from the new date. The list shows today's amount; a click shows every amount it has had.
  • ⟳ Bring up to date works out every due entry up to the day you pick and shows what it would do first: the new entries, entries whose amount changed (an amount added back in time - corrected when ticked) and entries no constant makes any more (removed only when ticked). ✔ Apply puts them in the ledger in date order - Date, Description, Category, Income or Expense, the running Balance and a Ref (📌 Rent · 2026-01-13) so nothing is entered twice. One Ctrl+Z takes it back.
  • The ledger is a sheet with Date and Income / Expense (or Amount) headings - yours, or ＋ Make a Ledger sheet. Rows you type yourself stay and are kept in date order with the rest.
  • Formulas read them: =Rent is today's amount, =CONST("Rent", A5) the amount on a date (a name with spaces: =Car_loan). They are saved with the workbook.
  • Paid to - what has been paid for a constant covers it day by day from its first start, a day being the weekly amount ÷ 7 (fortnightly ÷ 14, monthly × 12 ÷ 365, yearly ÷ 365). The list says Paid to 24/01/2026 · 3 days behind · $157.14 owing (or days ahead), and the ledger's Paid to column shows, on every row of a constant, the date paid to after that row - a live formula, =PAIDTO("Rent", amount paid). Payments are found by the Category (the constant's name) or the Ref. Untick ⟳ enters it for one whose payments you type yourself (a tenant's rent) - ⟳ then only fills in Paid to.

Tables

  • A→Z / Z→A sorts the table round the active cell by the active cell's column. A first row of headings stays on top, the cells of a row stay together (its formulas too), empty cells go last. A block that is picked is sorted on its own.
  • ⏷ Filter puts a ▾ on each heading - tick the values to show; the other rows are hidden (not deleted). Press it again to show every row.
  • ❄ Freeze keeps the rows above and the columns left of the active cell in view while you scroll.
  • Σ sums the numbers above the active cell (or to its left).

Sheets and files

  • The tabs under the grid are the workbook's sheets: ＋ adds one, a double-click renames one, a right-click copies, moves or deletes it.
  • 💾 Save (Ctrl+S) keeps the workbook under the name in the box; 📂 Open ▾ lists the workbooks in this project; 📄 New starts an empty one.
