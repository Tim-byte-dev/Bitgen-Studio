✉ Mail

Read and write your email in bitGen, with your own AI. bitGen talks to your mailbox itself - IMAP to read, SMTP to send - so there is no sign-up and no cloud service in between. The LLM you picked in the prompt docker reads your mail, flags what is important, sums emails up and drafts replies. The ✉ button on the top bar opens and closes the docker; it shows how many emails in your inbox are unread.

Adding your account (⚙ Accounts)

  • Type your email address - for Gmail, Outlook.com, Yahoo, iCloud, Fastmail, Zoho and GMX the servers fill themselves in; for other providers they are guessed (imap. / smtp. + your domain) - check them with your provider.
  • Password: Gmail, Outlook.com, Yahoo and iCloud refuse your normal password from a mail program - make an app password in your account's security settings and paste that. The password is kept encrypted on this computer (settings/keys.json) and is never shown again.
  • SSL ticked = encrypted from the start (incoming port 993, outgoing 465); unticked = STARTTLS (143 / 587).
  • ✓ Test logs in to both servers and sends nothing - when it logs in, the account is kept (2.820.0). Closing the dialog keeps what you typed too. Check the servers' certificates stays on unless your own server uses a self-made certificate.
  • Put a copy of sent mail in Sent - Gmail files sent mail itself, so it is off for Gmail.
  • A signature is added to new emails. Several accounts: pick one in the docker's account list.

Reading

  • Folders on the left, the emails in the middle (unread in bold), the email on the right. ↑ ↓ move through the list, Delete moves the email to Trash (in Trash it is deleted).
  • ★ flags the email for follow-up on the server, ● marks it unread / read.
  • Pictures from the internet are hidden until you press Show pictures - they can tell the sender you opened the email. Emails never run scripts here.
  • 📎 attachments download with a click. 🔍 filters the list as you type (sender, subject, the first words); Enter searches the whole folder on the server - from: to: subject: body: narrow it, quotes keep words together; ✕ goes back to the folder.
  • ⟳ Check mail checks now; bitGen also checks every few minutes while it is open (⚙ Accounts ▸ Check every).

Organising (2.820.0)

  • Several emails: Ctrl+click adds one, Shift+click takes every email between, Ctrl+A all of them, Esc none. 🗑, ★, ● and 📁 Move work on all the picked ones at once.
  • 📁 Move ▾ moves to another folder - or drag the emails onto a folder on the left.
  • ＋ beside the account's name makes a new folder on the server (Shift: inside the folder shown).
  • ⤓ Older emails at the end of the list reads 200 more of the folder from the server each time.
  • In To / Cc / Bcc the people in your mail are suggested as you type - ↑ ↓ and Enter (or a click) put one in.

✨ The AI

  • ✨ Flag with AI - the AI reads the emails it has not rated and puts a flag on each: red ⚑ urgent (needs you soon), orange ⚑ important (a person, your work, bills, appointments), blue ⚑ normal, grey ⚑ low (newsletters, offers, automated mail), with its reason under the subject. Shift+click rates them all again. ☐ ✨ auto = new mail is flagged as it comes in.
  • Tell it what matters to you (⚙ Accounts) - your clients, your accountant, the school - and those are rated higher.
  • Show ▸ ⚑ Important lists only the urgent and important ones; ✨ Most important first sorts by the flags.
  • ✨ Summarise - two sentences and what you need to do.
  • ✨ Draft reply / ✨ Reply all - type what the reply should say (or leave it empty), pick the tone, and the AI writes the reply in the composer. Read it through before you send - nothing goes until you press Send.

Writing (✉ New, ↩ Reply, ↩↩ Reply all, ↪ Forward)

  • ✨ Help me write - a few words or notes about what to say and a tone; ✨ Write it writes the email and its subject (in a reply: the reply).
  • ✨ Rewrite what I wrote… - shorter, friendlier, more formal, fix spelling and grammar, more confident, or your own instruction. ↶ Undo takes an AI change back.
  • Bold, italic, underline, bullets, numbering and links; 📎 Attach or drop files on the composer (up to 25 MB). A forward takes the original's attachments with it.
  • ➤ Send (Ctrl+Enter) or 💾 Save draft (into Drafts on the server).

Where things are kept

Your mail stays on your mail server. bitGen keeps a copy of what it has shown in the mail folder beside the app (this computer only - never in the repository or a release). Removing an account deletes that copy and the saved password. The AI runs where your LLM runs: on this computer, unless you set it to OpenRouter or a pod in the 🖥 GPU docker.
