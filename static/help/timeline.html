✨ Fill: generate inside a selection

Keyframes

  • ◀ ▶ jump between keyframes; the menu on a keyframe sets how it moves to the next one: linear, ease in, ease out, ease in+out, hold. ✕◆ deletes it. Click a ◆ on the timeline to edit it.
