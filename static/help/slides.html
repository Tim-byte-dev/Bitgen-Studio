🎞 Slides

Presentations in bitGen - slides with titles, points, pictures and shapes, shown full screen. The 🎞 button on the top bar opens and closes the docker (⛶ makes it big). Presentations are kept in your project's slides folder (a .bgk file); what you are doing is also kept in the browser, so closing the docker loses nothing.

Slides

  • The list on the left shows every slide. Click one to work on it; drag it up or down to move it; right-click for a new slide after it (pick its layout), Duplicate, Hide in the presentation, Move up / down, Delete.
  • ＋ Slide ▾ (or Ctrl+M) adds a slide after this one - pick its layout: Title slide, Title and content, Two contents, Section header, Picture with caption, Title only, Blank. ▤ Layout ▾ changes this slide's layout - its words go into the new layout's boxes.
  • 🎨 Theme ▾ gives the whole presentation its background, colours and fonts. Bg gives one slide its own background (↺ = the theme's again).

Text, shapes and pictures

  • An empty box says what goes in it ("Click to add a title"). Double-click a box (or pick it and press Enter) and type; Esc or a click outside it is done. Each line is a paragraph; in a box with bullets each line gets one.
  • 🅃 Text adds a text box (double-click an empty part of the slide does too). ⬚ Shape ▾: rectangle, rounded rectangle, ellipse, triangle, diamond, arrows, star, line - double-click a shape to type in it.
  • 🖼 Picture, a picture dropped on the slide or Ctrl+V of a copied picture. On a Picture with caption slide it fills the picture box. Right-click ▸ Fit / fill the box.
  • Click to pick, Shift / Ctrl+click for more, or drag a box round them on the empty slide. Drag to move - pink lines show when it lines up with the slide's middle or edges or with another item (Alt = no snapping, Shift = straight). The white squares size it (Shift keeps its shape; a picture keeps its shape unless Shift is held). Arrows nudge (Shift = 10 px).
  • The bar: font, size, B I U, colour, left / centre / right, ⇕ top / middle / bottom, •≡ bullets; a shape's Fill, Line and its width; ⇡ to the front, ⇣ to the back; 🗑 delete. Ctrl+D duplicates, Ctrl+C / X / V copy, cut and paste (also between slides and presentations), Ctrl+A picks everything, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo / redo.
  • Speaker notes go in the box under the slide.

Presenting

  • ▶ Present (F5) starts from the first slide, ▶ From here (Shift+F5) from this one - over the whole screen. Next: → ↓ Space Enter Page Down or a click; back: ← ↑ Backspace Page Up or a right-click; Home / End; a number then Enter jumps to that slide; B / W a black / white screen; Esc ends it. Hidden slides are left out.
  • Transition on the bar: how this slide comes in - None, Fade, Push, Wipe, Zoom.

Files

  • 📄 New ▾ - pick a theme and 16:9 or 4:3. 📂 Open ▾ lists this project's presentations. 💾 Save (Ctrl+S) keeps it under the name in the box.
  • ⤓ PowerPoint saves a .pptx - downloaded and kept in the slides folder: every slide with its background, text (fonts, sizes, colours, bullets), shapes, pictures and speaker notes.
