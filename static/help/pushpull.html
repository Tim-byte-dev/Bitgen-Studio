📏 PushPull

Model in 3D the quick way: draw a shape on the ground or on a face, then push or pull it into a solid. Sizes are typed at any moment into the Measurements box (bottom right) - no need to click there, just type and press Enter.

The tools (the letter is the key, with the pointer over the docker)

  • Space ⬚ Select - click a face or a line, Shift+click adds / takes out, Ctrl+A everything. A face of a group picks the whole group; double-click a group to edit inside it (the rest dims), Esc leaves. Delete erases what is picked.
  • L ╱ Line - click, click … A closed flat loop becomes a face. A line from one border of a face to another splits it in two (then push / pull one half).
  • R ▭ Rectangle - two opposite corners. Start it on a face and it lies in that face. Type 3,2 for 3 m × 2 m.
  • C ◯ Circle / N ⬠ Polygon - the centre, then the radius. Type the radius, or 36s for 36 sides.
  • P ⇧ Push/Pull - click a face, move, click (or drag). Type the distance. On its own a face becomes a solid; the top of a box makes the box taller; a shape drawn on a face goes in as a pocket or out as a boss; push a pocket's floor to the far side for a hole through. Just after a push, type a new distance to change it. Double-click a face = the last distance again.
  • M ✥ Move - pick something (or press on it), click a base point, click where it goes. Ctrl = a copy. Type the distance, or x,y,z.
  • Q ⟳ Rotate - pick, then click the centre, the start and the end (round the vertical axis, 15° steps). Type the angle.
  • S ⤡ Scale - pick, click a point and move away from / toward the middle. Type the factor.
  • F ⧈ Offset - click a face and move inside it: a loop that far in. Offset a floor and pull the ring up = walls.
  • T 📏 Tape - two points: their distance (and how far along each axis).
  • B 🪣 Paint - click faces (a group: all of it). Alt+click takes a face's colour. The colours are at the bottom.
  • E ⌫ Eraser - click or drag over lines and faces. A line that borders faces takes them with it.
  • O 🔄 Orbit, H ✋ Pan - in any tool the middle button orbits, the right button pans and the wheel zooms (toward the pointer). Shift+Z = everything in view.

Building - rooms, walls, doors, windows, roofs, lights

The Build tools under the modelling tools draw a building that stays editable: rooms keep their walls, floors and ceilings, and doors, windows and lights stay where they are when a room moves.

  • U ⌂ Room - two opposite corners (or type 4,3): walls, a floor and a ceiling. Its corners snap to other rooms' corners and wall middles. The panel on the right sets the next room's wall height, thickness, floor level and ceiling.
  • W ▮ Wall - click, click … a free-standing wall. Click its start again and it closes into a room. Double-click or Esc ends it. Type a length for the next piece.
  • D 🚪 Door - click on a wall: single, double, sliding glass, glass panel, or an opening without a door. Its middle snaps to the middle of the wall. Type 0.9,2.1 for its width and height.
  • I 🪟 Window - click on a wall: one pane, two panes or sliding, with its sill height. Type 1.2,1.2.
  • J ▦ Floor - two corners: a floor without walls (a deck, a patio, a storey's floor plate), at the height and thickness in the panel.
  • K ⌓ Ceiling - click a room: its ceiling on or off.
  • Y △ Roof - click a room: a roof over it (gable, hip, shed or flat, its pitch and overhang in the panel). Shift+click more rooms to put them under the same roof, or Roof the whole storey. Type a pitch in degrees.
  • X 💡 Light - pick the type in the panel: downlight (recessed), ceiling-mounted, pendant, chandelier, spot, wall light, floor lamp, table lamp; its colour (warm white to cool) and brightness in lumens. A click puts it on the ceiling of the room under the pointer (the room's middle snaps); a wall light goes on the wall you point at.
  • V ▟ Stairs - click the foot, then the direction and length (or type it). The height and width are in the panel.

Rooms share walls. With Select, press on a room and drag it: when one of its walls comes near another room's wall it snaps onto it, and the two rooms then have one wall between them. A door or window in that wall goes right through it. The corners line up too. Shift keeps the drag straight.

Drag a door or window along its wall (or onto another wall); drag a light, the stairs or a floor. Click anything built and the panel shows its settings - a room's size, wall height and thickness, the colours of its walls inside, its floor and ceiling, and of all the outside walls; ⟳ 90°, ⧉ Duplicate (a copy beside it that shares the wall), △ Roof. Delete erases what is picked; E Eraser erases with a click; B Paint paints a room's walls, a floor, a ceiling, a door or a roof.

🌙 Night turns the sun and sky down so the lights light the rooms (they are dark by day - a light here shines through walls, so by day it would light the outside too). ⌂ Cut-away hides every roof and ceiling to look in from above.

Inference - where the pointer snaps

  • The marker and its words show what the point is on: Endpoint, Midpoint, Origin, On Edge, On Face, On Ground.
  • From the last point, a dotted line shows when you are on the red / green / blue axis. Hold Shift to keep that axis, or press → red, ← green, ↑ blue (↓ lets it go).
  • Push / Pull snaps to the height of a corner, an edge or another face the pointer is over.
  • Lengths can be typed as 1.5 (m), 150cm, 1500mm, 5', 10".

The bar

  • ☰ File ▾ - New, Open, Save (Ctrl+S), Save as, Rename, bring in a 3D model of the project or a file from this computer (.pp3d, .glb, .gltf, .obj), Export, send to the image editor / the timeline / elsewhere, Delete, Maximize, Close.
  • The name and 💾 - models are kept in the project and save themselves a moment after each change. ↶ ↷ (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y).
  • View ▾ Iso / Top / Front / Right / Back / Left, Persp / Parallel, ⤢ everything in view, face style (shaded + edges, shaded, monochrome, X-ray, wireframe), ☀ shadows.
  • ▣ Group (G) what is picked; with a group picked it explodes it.
  • 🌙 Night and ⌂ Cut-away (see Building).
  • 🖼 → Image editor - a picture of the view as you see it (day or night). 🎞 → Timeline - the model as a 3D layer.
  • ➜ Send ▾: a picture of the view to the image editor (with or without the background), a 3D layer on the timeline (sent again, its layers update), the House builder, Blender, Unreal.
  • ⤓ Export ▾: .glb (with or without the lights - glTF lights, which Blender and Unreal bring in) or .obj into the project's 3D models; .glb, .obj or .stl (millimetres, for 3D printing) to this computer. ⤒ Import ▾: a 3D model of the project as a group.

⛶ Maximize (its title bar) fills the whole bitGen window under the title bar; 🗗 or Esc (when nothing else is going) puts it back.

The docker starts hidden - the PushPull button in the status bar shows it.
