Connect to my PC

On the PC: bitGen Studio ▸ Settings ▸ General ▸ Remote access ticked (the first time, restart bitGen Studio). Then press 📱 Connect the phone there - or press Allow on the bar that shows on the PC when this phone asks.

or with a QR / link
  1. On the PC: bitGen Studio ▸ Settings ▸ General ▸ Remote access - tick it (the first time, restart bitGen Studio).
  2. Press Create link - a QR code shows.
  3. Here: 📷 Scan the QR (take a photo of it), or paste the link under it.
or paste the link

The phone and the PC must be on the same Wi-Fi (not a guest network) to connect. Away from home the app reaches your PC through the bitGen relay for 6 hours after it was last on your Wi-Fi - sealed with a key only the app and your PC have.

Off: the style's own steps, CFG and size rules. On: these, as Basic Settings' Override style on the PC.

🎙 Talk

What you can say
  • find … / find out about … / search for … / look up … - your PC searches the web and answers (with the sources)
  • what is … / what's … / who … / when … / how much … / tell me about … - a question for your PC's LLM (with 🌐 Web on it searches when the answer needs it)
  • let's have a chat / let's talk / chat with me - 🤝 it chats as a friend; be my assistant - 🧑‍💼 back to your personal assistant
  • make a video of … - 🎬 the prompt filled in and Generate pressed
  • make a picture of … (photo, drawing, poster…) - 🖼 the same
  • make a song about … - 🎵 your PC writes it, then makes it
  • fix this photo / colourise this photo - 🩹 the picture you added on Create, through an edit style
  • anything else - a chat with your PC's LLM, as your assistant or your friend (the buttons above)

⚙ Settings

Disconnecting forgets the PC here. To pair again, create a new link on the PC. On the PC, Settings ▸ General ▸ Remote access ▸ Disconnect cuts this phone off from there.
