🎓 Life Choices - a new game

You start at 16 in high school. Answer quizzes, maths and word puzzles (grade 12 and up), choose college, a trade or the factory, find work, then buy property and shares once you have a steady income. Every turn is three months of your life.

❔ How to play Life Choices

Puzzles open in a box over the board with a 🧮 calculator beside it (➜ puts its result in the answer box). The dice you roll land in the centre circle.

High school (the centre): every square is a quiz, maths or word puzzle - right = money and IQ. Five right and you graduate; three wrong and you drop out (trades or the factory are still open).

The fork: 🎓 college (needs the diploma; exams at university level, six passes = a degree, three fails = out; tuition goes on a student debt), 🔧 trades (an apprenticeship, one square a turn, a small wage, ends with a licence) or 🏭 the factory (paid from day one, straight to the investment ring, but a low ceiling).

The jobs ring: job offers depend on your IQ and qualification; promotions are a puzzle; restructures can cost you your job. Night school takes you to college while you keep working.

The investment ring opens once you have a steady income (two paydays in a row). Buy property (tenants pay rent each turn; other players who land there pay you), buy and sell shares at the exchange, borrow from the bank (up to five times your salary). Short of cash? Shares and property are sold for you.

The land & houses ring (outside, through the 🗺 Land office): 🌳 blocks of land (no rent - build a house at a 🏗 Builder or when you land on your own block), 🏡 houses in good condition (full rent) and 🏚 houses that need renovating (cheap, little rent - renovate for a quarter of the value and it is worth half as much again). Council rates, and an auction at 85%.

Luck: every turn you roll a pair of dice. The total is your luck for those three months - double 1 (2) is the worst, 6 to 8 is an ordinary few months, double 6 (12) is the best and you roll again. At school it's lost bus passes and birthday gifts, at work fines and tax refunds, with money and property storms, inheritances and lottery wins. Inside, the first die moves you; on the investment and land & houses rings the pair's total moves you.

Bills, insurance and tax (once you work): every quarter rent (until you own a place), utilities, phone, getting around, food and fun - pick them in 💸 Budget on your turn (a picnic costs less than the cinema; a holiday most). Fun keeps your ❤ wellbeing up. 🏘 Share a house for about half the rent - but the same dice bring housemate luck, from a housemate who leaves owing rent to one who becomes a best friend. 🛡 Health, car and home insurance cost a premium each quarter; when bad luck hits something they cover, you pay only the excess. Pay is taxed on the 2026-27 Australian scale with the 2% Medicare levy; each birthday is a tax return - deductions (the $1,000 standard deduction, or loan interest, rates and repairs on your rentals) can bring a refund. A game's tax, not tax advice.

Winning: when everyone has retired, net worth + $1,000 for every IQ point over 100 + a bonus for your qualification + $2,000 for every ❤ wellbeing point over 50 (less below). "IQ" here is a game score, not a real IQ test.
